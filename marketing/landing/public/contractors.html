Are you a contractor?

Fill the document vault once and reuse it everywhere.

HazardLink gives you a vault for your insurance, SafePass and RAMS. Every tender you quote through the platform reuses the same documents, expiry dates are tracked so nothing lapses quietly, and clients see your paperwork is in order before you're on site.

To get started, ask any HazardLink client to invite you. You can opt in to this public directory from your profile whenever you're ready, and opt out again just as easily.
