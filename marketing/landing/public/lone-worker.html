Lone-worker safety

Someone should know when a lone worker goes quiet.

Night cleaners, single-guard sites and engineers on callout spend hours with nobody else around. HazardLink gives them timed check-ins on their phone, an escalation when a check-in is missed and a panic alarm for the moments that can't wait.

How it works

Check-ins that notice when they stop.

The worker keeps working. The app only makes noise when something is off.

1

Start a session

The worker starts a lone-worker session on their phone when they begin, and picks the check-in interval that fits the job.

2

Check in as they go

The app prompts before each check-in falls due. Confirming takes a tap, so it doesn't get in the way of the actual work.

3

A missed check-in escalates

If the time passes with no response, the people you've chosen are alerted with who was working, the site they were on and when they last confirmed, so someone follows up straight away.

Beyond the timer

A panic alarm for the worst case.

Alongside the timed check-ins there's a panic alarm in the same app. Triggering it raises an immediate alert to your escalation contacts rather than waiting for the next check-in to lapse.

Night cleaners

Finishing floors in an empty building, often the last person out. A missed check-in at 2am should wake somebody up.

Security guards

Single-guard sites and quiet night shifts. The same app already runs their patrols and incident reports, so cover for the guard comes built in.

Solo engineers

Plant rooms, roofs and callouts at odd hours. The riskiest work often happens where nobody would hear a shout.

Duty of care

Working alone is legal. Ignoring the risk isn't.

The Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005 expects employers to assess the hazards their staff face and put sensible controls in place, and that duty doesn't shrink because the worker is on their own. For someone working alone, a documented check-in arrangement is exactly that kind of control.

A check-in system is one control inside a wider lone-working risk assessment, and it works best alongside proper training and sensible rostering.

The record

If you're ever asked, the log answers.

  • Every session, check-in and escalation is logged with times
  • Escalations go to the contacts you choose
  • Alerts arrive by email and in-app today, with SMS and WhatsApp ready to switch on as they're configured
  • Lone-worker records sit beside your incidents and patrols in the same system
