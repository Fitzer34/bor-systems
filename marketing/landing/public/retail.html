Spill response

The sign raises the alarm itself.

Our IoT wet-floor signs detect when they're lifted and put down. From that moment the response is tracked, timed and escalated if nobody moves.

A live alert with a timer

The moment a sign is deployed, an alert goes up with the time and the place, and an escalation timer starts. If nobody responds in time, it moves up the chain.

The floor plan shows it live

Every sign's status sits on your floor plan, so the duty manager sees the state of the building at a glance rather than over the radio.

A cleaner closes it with a photo

The nearest cleaner is dispatched and logs the resolution with a photo. The job keeps its times: detected, responded, resolved.

The record

If a claim arrives, the file is already there.

Slip claims tend to surface long after the mop has dried. Because the response was tracked while it happened, the times, the photos and who did what are already on the job, ready to hand to your insurer.

  • Response times recorded on every spill, automatically
  • Photo proof of the resolution, logged by the cleaner
  • Incident reports with photos and severity for anything bigger
  • An audit log behind it all, showing who did what and when

Reporting

Staff and shoppers can report without an app.

A QR code at the customer service desk or on a column lets anyone report a spill or a fault on the spot. There's no login, and the report becomes a tracked job with a photo attached.

Audit pack

One audit pack per site.

When an insurer, a landlord or head office asks how the site is run, export the audit pack: spill response times, incidents, inspections, the chemical register, certificates and the audit log in one printable document.

The rest of the centre runs on the same platform: work orders and planned maintenance for the fabric of the building, patrols with QR checkpoints for the security team, and visitor sign-in at the desk.
