How it works

From a code on the wall to a job in the queue.

The person who finds the fault does the reporting, on the spot, with the phone already in their hand.

1

Print and place

Create a QR code for a room, a corridor or a piece of equipment and print it on any office printer. Stick it where people will see it.

2

Scan and describe

Anyone points their phone camera at the code. A short form opens in the browser where they describe the fault and can add a photo. No app, no account.

3

It becomes a job

The report lands with your team as a work order against that exact room or asset, with the photo attached and the time recorded.

The record

A proper trail from the first scan.

A scanned report follows the same path as a job your own staff would log, so it gets tracked and closed like any other piece of work.

  • A tracked work order with a status, an owner and a history
  • Photos from the person who found the fault
  • The exact room or asset, identified by the code itself
  • Time stamps from report through to resolution
  • A full trail you can show an auditor or an insurer later
