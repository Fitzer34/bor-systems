Documented work

Cleaning, incidents and maintenance, all on the record.

The record is a by-product of the work itself, so nobody has to write the day up twice.

Cleaning you can show

Rounds are confirmed room by room as they happen, with photos where they're needed, and the log builds itself.

Incidents followed up

A trip hazard, a leak or a broken handrail is recorded with photos and severity, and each report carries its follow-up trail to closure.

Maintenance on schedule

An asset register with planned maintenance schedules, so servicing is scheduled rather than remembered, and every work order keeps its history.

HazardLink looks after the building and the work done in it. Care planning and clinical records stay in the systems you already use for them.

Chemical register

Safety data sheets from a barcode scan.

Scan the barcode on a cleaning product and its safety data sheet joins your chemical register. The extraction is done by AI and then verified by a person before it stands, so the register reflects the sheet itself and nothing invented.

Certificates

Expiry dates tracked, lapses flagged.

Contractors coming on site keep their insurance, SafePass and RAMS in a reusable document vault, with expiry dates tracked so out-of-date paperwork is flagged rather than discovered after the fact.
