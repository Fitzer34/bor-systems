HANGER: the sign hangs from the bar under the bottom edge. Nothing on the face except the screen window, PRG button, RST pinhole,
LED window and the label. A web at the back carries the bar 30 mm below the body so the sign's top sits in the pocket between bar
and box; the bar reaches 70 mm out from the wall face and ends in a 20 mm lip; the sign's handle settles in the saddle 2 mm
in front of the lid face, directly over the Hall sensor. The three Hall leads come up the web into the body through the bottom-wall
slot and run forward in the 3 mm gap under the battery holder to the board.

HANGER SERVICE SEQUENCE (unit stays on the wall)
  1. Remove the four M3 pin-Torx countersunk lid screws. Lift the lid straight off (the lip is 4 mm deep).
  2. The 21700 cell sits in its holder facing you: push it against the spring end and lift it out. Fit the new cell, same polarity.
  3. To swap the board: unplug the JST 1.25 battery lead (under the board, plug enters from the right), unplug the U.FL antenna,
     unsolder or cut the three Hall wires at the board underside pads (J3 pin 1 GND, pin 2 3V3, pin 17 GPIO6), lift the board
     off its rails. No screws hold the board: it rests on the rails and is clamped by the two lid ribs.
  4. To swap the Hall sensor: lift the sign off, remove the two M3 button-head screws under the bar, drop the bar off its key,
     slide the Hall carrier out of the bar's back end, feed the new leads up the web, through the bottom-wall slot and forward
     under the holder.
  5. Refit in reverse. Insert the cell last, close the lid within the 60 s commissioning window, or press RST (2 mm pinhole) later.
  To take the unit off the wall: with the lid off, remove the internal M3 screw at (50, 62) through the back wall, then lift the body
  14 mm and pull it off the four backplate pegs.

GATEWAY SERVICE SEQUENCE
  1. Remove the six M3 countersunk lid screws, lift the lid.
  2. Unplug the USB-C plug (cable stays tied to the saddle), unplug the U.FL pigtail, lift the board off its rails.
